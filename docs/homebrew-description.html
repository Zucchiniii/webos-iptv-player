webOS IPTV Player, redesigned, by Zucchiniii: github.com/Zucchiniii/webos-iptv-player

A fast IPTV player for LG webOS TVs, rebuilt for big playlists and the 10-foot screen. Plays M3U playlists and Xtream Codes accounts, with an XMLTV program guide, catch-up and timeshift.

Highlights

  • Find channels fast: a channel grid with country chips. Your TV's own country comes first.
  • Never cut off: while you watch, a card next to the channel list shows the focused channel's full name, what's on, the description and what's next.
  • Stable with huge lists: movies and series mixed into live lists are filtered out, and a crash while loading reopens in safe mode.
  • Sharpest picture: up to 2160p on a 4K TV, starting at full quality.
  • Instant search across channels, programs, movies and series.
  • Stays on your channel: if a stream drops, the app retries it instead of switching channels.
  • Movies and series with resume, subtitles (including online search), reminders, parental controls and 16 themes.

Updates arrive through Homebrew Channel. Installing over the original app keeps your playlists and settings.

Based on webOS IPTV Player by lennylxx (Apache 2.0).
